Telegram Digest (twice daily via Railway Cron)
--------------------------------------------

1) Set env vars in Railway:
   - TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN
   - TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID
   - DIGEST_TOKEN  (choose a random secret string)

2) Add two Railway cron jobs (HTTP GET):
   - /internal/telegram-digest-cron?token=<DIGEST_TOKEN> at 07:00 Europe/Athens
   - /internal/telegram-digest-cron?token=<DIGEST_TOKEN> at 14:20 Europe/Athens

Manual test (admin only):
   POST /internal/telegram-digest
